Save Wilder

Lake Washington School District is deciding which schools to consolidate or close. We're asking LWSD to evaluate alternatives first, using current enrollment and housing data, the full costs of closure, and the impact on children, families and existing programs.

What we're asking LWSD

Look at every option before closing a school

  1. 1

    Fill Wilder first

    Evaluate boundary adjustments and program placements that bring Wilder to 80–90% of its permanent capacity before considering closure.

  2. 2

    Use current data

    Make sure projections reflect recent home sales. Many longtime owners in our neighborhoods have sold to young families with children not yet in school.

  3. 3

    Publish the full cost

    Show the net savings of any closure after added busing, upkeep of an empty building, costs at receiving schools and families who leave the district.

  4. 4

    Weigh the impact on kids and families

    Wilder is the only LWSD elementary in Woodinville, and its nearest neighbors are more than three miles away by road. Closure would put our littlest learners on long bus rides and disrupt existing programs, including Wilder's preschool classrooms.

How you can help

Three things you can do this week

1. Sign the petition

It takes about 30 seconds. Parents, alumni, staff and neighbors are all welcome. There's a box on the form if you'd like to volunteer for canvassing or outreach.

Sign at savewilder.com/sign →

2. Send a short, personal email

A few sentences in your own words carry more weight than a form letter. Send it to all three:

boardmembers@lwsd.orgLWSD School Board, who make the final decision
superintendent@lwsd.orgSuperintendent Dr. Jon Holmen
BoundaryCommittee@lwsd.orgBoundary & Enrollment Realignment Committee

Tips for writing it

  • Say who you are: a Wilder parent, alum, staff member or neighbor.
  • Share one specific story about what Wilder means to your family.
  • Make one clear ask, such as "evaluate alternatives before closing Wilder."
  • Keep it under 200 words and keep the tone respectful. District staff are reading a lot of email.

Sample to adapt

Subject: Please keep Wilder Elementary open

Dear Dr. Holmen and Board Directors,

My name is [your name], and [my child is in __ grade at Wilder / I live in the Wilder neighborhood]. I understand LWSD is facing declining enrollment, and I support a careful realignment process.

I'm asking you to keep Wilder open by first evaluating alternatives, such as boundary adjustments or program placements that fill Wilder's empty permanent seats. Please also publish the full net cost of any closure, including added busing.

[One or two sentences about what Wilder means to your family.]

Thank you for your work on this.

[Your name]
[Neighborhood]

Please leave out: criticism of other schools, home values, and anything suggesting the decision has already been made. These make it easier for a letter to be set aside.

Questions

Common questions

Has Wilder been chosen for closure?

No. No school has been named. The district has said each learning area should expect some change, and that one or more schools could be identified for closure. That's why we're speaking up now, while options are still being evaluated.

Who makes the decision?

The district's Boundary & Enrollment Realignment Committee and the superintendent develop a recommendation. The elected school board takes it up on March 22, 2027.

Why not just move Wilder students to schools with space?

Wilder is the only LWSD elementary in Woodinville, and every alternative is more than three miles away by road. Closing it moves our families farther than most closures would, adds bus costs, and risks families leaving the district altogether.

I don't have kids at Wilder. Can I still help?

Yes. Neighbors, alumni and future Wilder families are welcome to sign the petition and email the district.

Is this an official district page?

No. This page is run by Wilder parents and neighbors and isn't affiliated with Lake Washington School District.
